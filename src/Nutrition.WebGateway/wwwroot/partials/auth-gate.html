1. Wellness Companion & Non-Medical Scope

Diet Dost is an artificial intelligence-driven dietary and lifestyle tracking companion designed in adherence to ICMR-NIN 2024 recommendations. Diet Dost is not a hospital, clinic, licensed physician, or prescription provider. Recommendations generated by Diet Dost are for informational and educational wellness purposes only. You must always consult a licensed medical practitioner prior to initiating substantial caloric deficits or dietary modifications.

2. AI Model Training & Data License

By registering, you grant Diet Dost a worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual, non-exclusive license to utilize de-identified and anonymized meal photographs, dish descriptions, portion estimates, user correction records, and app telemetry strictly for internal research, algorithmic benchmarking, and training/fine-tuning machine learning models (including Small Language Models and Multimodal Vision Models).

3. Partner Sharing & Research

Diet Dost may share de-identified and aggregated nutritional cohorts with certified clinical dietitians, academic research institutions, and technology partners to improve Indian and South Asian dietary intelligence.

4. Statutory DPDPA 2023 Compliance

All personal data is processed under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023). You retain the right to erase your account and all associated health records at any time.

DPDPA 2023 §6 Compliance

Sensitive Clinical Health Processing Consent

1. Processing of Sensitive Health Data

Under Section 6 of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), you explicitly consent to the collection, cryptographic storage, and computational processing of your biometric markers (height, weight, biological sex, age), clinical health conditions (e.g. Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, PCOS, Hypothyroidism), and pharmaceutical medications.

2. Purpose Limitation

Your health data is processed solely to calculate personalized Mifflin-St Jeor metabolic equations, ICMR-NIN 2024 macronutrient ratios, glycemic load alerts, and potential drug-nutrient interaction warnings.

3. Right to Erasure

In accordance with DPDPA 2023 §12, you retain the right to erase your account and all associated health records at any time via the self-service Account Deletion tool.

4. Security & Isolation Standard

Your records are secured using AES-256 / PBKDF2 encryption and isolated at the tenant claim level. Third-party advertisers and data brokers have zero access to your health metrics.
