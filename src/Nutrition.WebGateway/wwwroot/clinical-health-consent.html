Clinical Health & Nutrition Processing Consent

Regulatory Act: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) §6
Data Fiduciary: Diet Dost Health Technologies
Purpose: Metabolic & Clinical Nutrition Governance

1. Statutory Basis & Explicit Unbundled Consent

In accordance with Section 6 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA 2023) and international health privacy frameworks, your consent to process sensitive personal health data must be clear, affirmative, explicit, informed, and strictly unbundled from general terms of service.

LEGAL MANDATE: By checking the "Clinical Health & Nutrition Processing" checkbox during registration or profile setup, you provide affirmative, tamper-evident consent for Diet Dost to ingest, compute, and store the specific categories of biometric and medical information set forth below.

2. Categories of Health & Clinical Data Collected

Diet Dost collects and computes strictly the following health data points necessary for precision South Asian clinical dietetics:

  • Biometric Markers: Height (cm / ft+in), Current Body Weight (kg), Target Weight (kg), Age, and Biological Sex.
  • Body Composition Indices: WHO South Asian Body Mass Index (BMI cutoffs: normal 18.5–22.9, overweight 23.0–24.9, obese $\ge 25.0$ kg/m²), Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) via Mifflin-St Jeor formulas, and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).
  • Diagnosed Medical Conditions: Self-declared diagnoses including Type 2 Diabetes, Hypertension, PCOS, Hypothyroidism, Dyslipidemia, Hyperuricemia / Gout, Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD), and Chronic Kidney Disease.
  • Pharmaceutical Prescriptions & Active Regimens: Self-declared medication schedules (e.g., Metformin, Telmisartan, Ramipril, Atorvastatin, Thyroxine) to evaluate drug-nutrient interactions.
  • Visual Transformation Progression: User-uploaded face and body progress photos used to calculate visual progress and weight delta milestones.

3. Purpose Limitation & Prohibited Uses

In strict adherence to the Purpose Limitation Principle (DPDPA 2023 §6(1)):

  • Permitted Clinical Calculations: Your health markers are used solely to establish your personalized ICMR-NIN 2024 caloric budget, macronutrient targets (protein, carbohydrate, fat, dietary fiber ratios), daily sodium ceilings (< 2,000 mg/day WHO limit), and clinical alerts (e.g. potassium cautions when combining ARB/ACE inhibitors with coconut water).
  • Zero Health Advertising / Third-Party Brokerage: Diet Dost will NEVER sell, rent, license, or broker your personal health data to health insurance underwriters, pharmaceutical marketers, advertising networks, or consumer credit bureaus.

4. Cryptographic Storage & Forensic Consent Audit Trail

To ensure evidentiary defense and prevent consent repudiation (DPDPA 2023 §6(10)):

  • Every consent transaction cryptographically logs the precise UTC timestamp, consent version string, user GUID, remote IP address, and browser User-Agent string in an immutable audit ledger.
  • All stored health profiles are encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3). Application databases enforce strict claim-based tenant isolation so no user can access another user's clinical profile or progress photos.

5. Your Rights as a Data Principal (DPDPA 2023 §11–14)

As a Data Principal, you possess statutory rights enforceable under Indian law:

  • Right to Access & Summary (Section 11): You may view your complete health ledger, nutrient logs, and profile records at any time.
  • Right to Correction & Updating (Section 12): You can modify or update your biometrics, diagnosed conditions, and medications directly in your Profile.
  • Right to Erasure & Account Purge (Section 12): You may permanently delete your account and all associated medical data at any time via POST /api/auth/delete-account.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13): Inquiries and complaints can be directed to the designated Data Protection Officer at dpo@dietdost.app.
